The filter

How we choose.

A catalog is only useful if it leaves things out. These are the three tests every app has to pass.

01 / USEFUL

It has to solve something.

Not another wrapper around the same API. Not a demo with a download button. It has to be the tool you reach for twice.

Useful before clever
02 / RESPECTFUL

No account tax.

No sign-up wall between you and the download. No bundleware, no silent extras, no ‘recommended” installs. The app does its job and gets out of the way.

No accounts, no tricks
03 / HONEST

The limits are stated.

Downloads start directly from the app page. Warnings are explained before you click, and the copy doesn’t promise more than the software can do.

Receipts over slogans

Built in the open

A catalog, not a content farm.

Each app keeps its own repository, release notes, license and download page. Asraab is the index — not a middleman.

  • 01
    One app, one repository.

    Updates and issue reports live with the app that ships them. No central release bundle to untangle.

  • 02
    No tracking, no account.

    The site is static, makes no third-party requests, and stores only your theme and language preference.

  • 03
    Apps can leave.

    If an app stops meeting the bar, it comes out of the catalog. Small is the point, not a phase.
