Extension privacy

Privacy policy

Download with Tayyar browser extension · Last updated: October 22, 2026

Download with Tayyar connects your browser to the Tayyar desktop app on your computer. It has no developer-run servers and no analytics.

Information we handle

The extension accesses download links, filenames, referring page URLs, browser information, and cookies relevant to downloads. Cookies may include sign-in information needed to download protected files. It also reads tab URLs to provide website-specific controls and close empty download tabs.

How we use it

This information is sent directly to the Tayyar app on your computer to handle downloads. The extension does not send it to the developer’s servers, sell it, or use it for advertising or tracking. There is no analytics SDK and no remote logging.

Storage and controls

Extension settings are stored locally in your browser and are not synced. The extension does not save a separate copy of your cookies. You can disable automatic capture, exclude websites, or uninstall the extension to remove its local settings. Download history and app settings live on your PC, managed by Tayyar.

Limited Use

The extension’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the Tayyar GitHub issue tracker. Please do not include cookies or private download links in public issues.

Tayyar is free and open source under the MIT license, based on Plexo. The extension source is available in the Tayyar repository.
